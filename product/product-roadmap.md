# Product Roadmap

## Sections

### 1. Job Board
Public job listings with search, filters and job detail pages, plus the apply flow where signed-in candidates send a CV and cover letter.

### 2. Candidate Portal
Where job seekers sign up, sign in, keep a basic profile and CV, and track each application through a dated status history.

### 3. Admin Portal
The internal staff area where the EduBridge team posts and manages every job, reviews applications and moves candidates through hiring stages, and follows up on enquiries.

### 4. Info Pages
Public For Employers, Contact and FAQ pages, where employers and visitors learn how Recruiture works and send enquiries to the EduBridge team.
