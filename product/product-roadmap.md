# Product Roadmap

## Sections

### 1. Job Board
Public job listings with search, filters and job detail pages, plus the apply flow that lets candidates send a CV and cover letter.

### 2. Candidate Portal
Where job seekers build their profile, upload a CV, save jobs, track their applications and show their EduBridge graduate badge.

### 3. Employer Dashboard
Where employers manage their company profile, post and edit jobs, and move applicants through a hiring pipeline.

### 4. Recruiter and Admin
Tools for the EduBridge team to manage client roles and shortlists, verify graduates, verify companies and moderate jobs and users.
